Context management

Context is the meeting memory that lets each turn build on the previous one. It includes participant messages, route state, selected speakers, and turn metadata.

What stays in context

The engine carries forward the messages and structured state needed for the next participant to respond coherently. This is why speaker order, route proposals, and refinement results can influence later turns.

Cycles and balanced turns

When balanced turns are on, a cycle means every participant gets one turn before anyone repeats. When balanced turns are off, the engine no longer needs a cycle boundary and can choose the next speaker more freely.

Logs and review

Use the live logs to understand how context is changing. They show the selected speaker, generated messages, errors, and other events that explain why the meeting moved the way it did.
