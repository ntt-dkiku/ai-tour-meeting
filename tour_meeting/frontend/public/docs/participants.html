Participants

Participants are the voices in a meeting. They can be AI agents with their own roles and prompts, or a human participant inserted into the turn order.

Participant roles

Give each participant a clear role so the meeting produces useful contrast. For example, one participant can focus on route quality, another can challenge assumptions, and another can summarize tradeoffs.

Ordering participants

Drag participants into the order you want before starting. Round robin uses this order directly. Random can either preserve balance across a cycle or choose freely when balanced turns are off.

Human participant

A human participant lets you join the meeting yourself. The app keeps a placeholder for that participant internally, then removes or inserts it as needed when the backend receives the final order.
