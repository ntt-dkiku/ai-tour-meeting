Meeting workflow

This page describes how a tour meeting proceeds and the workflow options that control it.

Overall workflow

The meeting proceeds according to the specified turn rule, under which participants sequentially take actions multiple times within their turns. This normal state is referred to as the conversation phase.

Once a participant proposes an itinerary (i.e., terminates their turn with propose), the meeting transitions to the voting phase. In this phase, all participants except the proposer cast votes on the proposed itinerary. After collecting all votes, the acceptance or rejection of the proposal is determined in accordance with the specified voting rule. If the itinerary is accepted, the current itinerary is replaced with the proposed one; if it is rejected, the current itinerary remains unchanged. The meeting then returns to the conversation phase, and the turns resume with the next participant after the proposer.

When all participants consecutively indicate satisfaction with the current itinerary (i.e., terminate their turns with satisfied), consensus is considered reached and the meeting concludes. In practice, the termination condition may also be constrained by a maximum number of turns or a maximum execution time, in addition to the unanimous agreement.

Turn rules

The turn rule determines the speaking order of the participants. Note that different turn rules may be applied separately to the conversation and voting phases.

round_robin

Rotates participants in a fixed cyclic order.

random

Rotates participants randomly.

inviting

Lets the current speaker determine the next speaker.

facilitating

Assigns an additional turn to a facilitator after each turn, who then determines the next speaker.

parallel (voting phase only)

Lets all participants vote simultaneously.

Turn options

Two additional options make the turn rules more flexible:

Balancing

Organizes speaking turns into cycles such that all participants take turns once per cycle to ensure fairness.

Volunteer

Allows participants to select pass as a terminal action and skip their turn (i.e., only those who wish to speak take a turn).

Voting rules

The voting rule determines how the votes decide the acceptance or rejection of a proposed itinerary. In the first three rules, participants cast a binary vote (accept or reject); in the last two, participants assign a score to the proposed itinerary according to 10-point rubrics.

majority

The proposed itinerary replaces the current itinerary if it receives a majority of accept votes.

unanimous

The proposed itinerary replaces the current itinerary only if all participants vote accept.

single_decider

A specified participant alone casts the binary vote, and the current itinerary is updated accordingly.

most_pleasure

The proposed itinerary replaces the current itinerary if the sum of all participants' scores is greater than or equal to that of the current itinerary.

least_misery

The proposed itinerary replaces the current itinerary if the minimum score across participants is greater than or equal to that of the current itinerary.
